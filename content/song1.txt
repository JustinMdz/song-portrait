The afternoon got caught inside your window
smelling like the rain and something on the stove
and while the whole world hurries past the curtain
I stay right here and count the things I know

Oranges on the table in the kitchen
your laughter coming down the stairs again
if time should ever ask me where I wandered
I'll say that I was home, and home was then

And if I forget it all
let me not forget this part
the light that came in slowly
and your head against my heart

I kept your footsteps in a paper notebook
your songs inside the mirror of the car
and every Sunday that refuses ending
still carries how you sounded from afar

We grew old in a single afternoon
we grew young again when evening came
and on the wall your shadow stayed behind us
dancing by itself, without a name

And if I forget it all
let me not forget this part
the light that came in slowly
and your head against my heart

And if I forget myself
let someone tell me, word by word
there was an orange afternoon
the sweetest thing I ever heard
